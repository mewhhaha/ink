# Generated dungeon materials

These seamless albedo textures were generated for the Ink game-development
example on 2026-08-24, then resized to 512×512 and stripped of metadata:

- `limestone-wall.png`: warm gray-beige hand-cut limestone blocks with subtle
  mortar, flat neutral illumination, and seamless wrapping.
- `flagstone-floor.png`: cool charcoal worn flagstone slabs with restrained
  variation, flat neutral illumination, and seamless wrapping.
- `oak-door.png`: dark vertical oak planks with black iron straps and rivets,
  flat neutral illumination, and seamless wrapping.

They contain no baked directional lighting. Floors and walls use Ink's
world-space triplanar projection; the oak image uses a calibrated local
projection so its planks and ironwork follow the Kenney gate model.
