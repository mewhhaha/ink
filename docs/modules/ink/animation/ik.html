ink/animation/ik

3 types · 9 values

Analytic inverse kinematics on world-space joints and rotations. Callers own the rig, the targets and every clock; results are fresh values. Directions are world vectors (a pole is pole_position - root). Angles are radians. Nonfinite inputs yield Nothing.

Key items: Chain, Axes, CcdResult, limit_rotation, fraction.

Types

data Chain 
type Chain is data

New joints and the world-space rotations that carry the old bones onto them: bone 1 turns by root_delta; bone 2 then turns by mid_delta (apply mid_delta * root_delta * old_rotation to bone 2).

type Axes is data

Local bone axes the constraint points at the target (and, optionally, keeps upright). A zero up selects a swing-only aim with no twist control.

func limit_rotation 
func limit_rotation: Quat -> F32 -> Quat

Shortest-arc rotation, nonnegative w, of at most limit radians.

func fraction 
func fraction: Quat -> F32 -> Quat

A fraction t in [0,1] of the shortest rotation; exact at both ends.

func soft_reach 
func soft_reach: F32 -> F32 -> F32 -> F32 -> F32

Reach after a soft limit: identity up to high - soft * high, then a C1 rational approach that never exceeds high. Soft zero is a hard clamp. The reach never falls below low.

func two_bone 
func two_bone: Vec3 -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> F32 -> F32 -> Maybe Chain

Root/mid/end joints reach for target, bending toward pole. Bone lengths are preserved for every weight. Targets beyond the (soft) reach straighten the chain toward the target; targets nearer than |l1-l2| fold it fully. A pole parallel to the reach axis keeps the current bend side, then picks a deterministic perpendicular. Weight blends the bone directions along their shorter arcs, so lengths still hold; zero returns the input pose.

func aim_rotation 
func aim_rotation: Quat -> Axes -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> F32 -> F32 -> Maybe Quat

Rotation of rotation after turning its axes toward direction, with at most limit radians of total turn and weight of that turn applied. A zero direction keeps the rotation. The result is a world rotation.

func aim_chain 
func aim_chain: #[Quat] -> #[F32] -> Axes -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> F32 -> F32 -> Maybe #[Quat]

Aim the last rotation of a chain (spine to neck to head) and spread the turn over every bone by shares (nonnegative; a bone takes its share of what remains, so the tip reaches the aim exactly unless limited or its share is zero). Rotations and results are world rotations, root first; a bone's result already includes the turn of the bones above it.

func fabrik 
func fabrik: #[Vec3] -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> #[F32] -> U32 -> F32 -> F32 -> Maybe #[Vec3]

Multi-bone FABRIK (Forward And Backward Reaching Inverse Kinematics). positions are world joint positions from root (index 0) to end effector. cone_limits optionally caps each joint's bend angle (radians) relative to the preceding segment direction (empty for unconstrained). Preserves every bone length and clamps unreachable targets straight along target - root.

func chain_rotations 
func chain_rotations: #[Vec3] -> #[Vec3] -> #[Quat] -> Maybe #[Quat]

Compute world bone rotations that carry rest_positions segments onto solved_positions segments from rest_rotations.

func ccd 
func ccd: #[Vec3] -> #[Quat] -> Vec3 -> #[F32] -> U32 -> F32 -> F32 -> Maybe CcdResult

Multi-bone CCD (Cyclic Coordinate Descent) IK. Iterates from the penultimate joint back to the root, rotating the sub-chain toward target. Optional limits (radians, empty for unconstrained) cap each joint's total deviation from its input rotation.
