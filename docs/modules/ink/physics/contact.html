ink/physics/contact

1 types · 30 values

The narrow phase: contact points between two shapes. A contact has a world position, a normal leading from the first shape to the second, and a separation: negative when the shapes overlap, positive but within the margin when they are close (speculative contacts let a solver stop a body exactly at a surface instead of after it has sunk in).

Key items: Contact, sphere, is_sphere, flip, known.
