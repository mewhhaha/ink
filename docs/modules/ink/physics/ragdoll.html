ink/physics/ragdoll

1 types · 14 values

Ragdolls from an animation skeleton. A skeleton is the parent array and the local bind poses animation uses (U32 max marks roots; parents precede children). Each bone becomes one dynamic capsule body from the bone origin to its tail (bone-local), and each non-root bone gets a cone-twist joint at its origin. Body slot of bone i is first + i; entity id id_base + i. Bone scales are ignored (unit scale). Builders return empty arrays for an invalid skeleton. Give a ragdoll one layer and leave it out of its own mask to disable self collision (neighbouring capsules overlap at every joint).

Key items: Bone, default_bones, valid_bone, valid_pose, valid.

swing is the cone half-angle and twist_low..twist_high the twist range about the bone axis (radians); strength is the maximum drive torque (Nm).
