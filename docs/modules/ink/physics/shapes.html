ink/physics/shapes

11 types · 59 values

Collision shapes in world space, and the geometry under them.

A shape is a capsule (a segment with a radius; a sphere when the segment is a point), a finite heightfield, or a convex polytope (Poly): boxes and brushes. A Poly keeps its vertices, its outward face planes n . p <= d (the same convention as Ink's brushes), the vertex ring of every face, and its edges, so the narrow phase can clip faces and find closest features exactly.

Points and planes use explicit structural scalar rows for their packed geometry schema. Geometry aliases independently named resizable owners.

Key items: Point3, Plane4, Ring, Poly, read.
