ink/ai/steering

3 types · 20 values

Desired velocity and acceleration are separate so callers choose integration.

Key items: Flock, Neighbourhood, Flocking, limit, seek.

Types

data Flock 
type Flock is data

Boids: members' positions, velocities and groups (parallel arrays; extra entries in a longer one are ignored), bucketed in cells of size (spatial.cells) for neighbourhood queries. A size near the query radius visits fewest candidates. Rebuild it from the members' state each step. It also keeps the members' places and groups in the cells' order (xs, ys, zs, kinds), which a query reads straight through.

alias Neighbourhood 
type Neighbourhood = { count: U32, cx: F32, cy: F32, cz: F32, hx: F32, hy: F32, hz: F32, px: F32, py: F32, pz: F32 }

What one member makes of the others: of those within the radius in its own group, how many, their centre and their mean velocity (zero for none); and the separation push (as separation, unlimited) from every member closer than the spacing, any group. Coincident members push along a direction about the vertical fixed by the member's index, so a stack parts. A flat record of the count and the centre (cx, cy, cz), heading (hx, hy, hz) and push (px, py, pz), so a query returns one small object rather than one per vector; centre, heading and push give the vectors.

data Flocking 
type Flocking is data

How flock members steer. Gains (per second) on the goal (toward the desired velocity), alignment (toward the group's mean velocity), cohesion (toward its centre, per second squared per metre) and separation (on the push); the most acceleration, the top speed, the fastest turn in radians a second, and how far in radians the heading may stray from the goal's direction (pi or more leaves it free; less keeps a member with a goal making headway, so it cannot circle it).

func flocking 
func flocking: Flocking -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> { count: U32, cx: F32, cy: F32, cz: F32, hx: F32, hy: F32, hz: F32, px: F32, py: F32, pz: F32 } -> F32 -> Vec3

A member's velocity after seconds, from its position and velocity, its own desired velocity goal (from seek, arrive or a wander) and its neighbourhood: the weighted forces, limited to the most acceleration, then held within the stray of the goal, within the turn of the old velocity and under the top speed. Always finite: with a non-finite input it keeps the old velocity, under the top speed, or stops.

func avoid 
func avoid: Scene -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> F32 -> F32 -> U32 -> U32 -> Vec3

The change to add to a member's goal (its desired velocity, before flocking) that keeps a sphere of radius at position off the scene's solids. One cast of the sphere along velocity for ahead seconds, with scene's solid filter: mask layers, never entity skip, no triggers. Zero for a clear way, no motion or a non-finite input. A hit at distance d of the reach L has the urgency u = 1 - d / L. The change takes u of the goal's part into the surface away and turns it along the surface, so a member slides along a wall rather than stopping: the way the member already slides, else the way the goal leans, else to its right (about the vertical, as within_angle; about x for a vertical normal). It pushes out along the normal by u times the goal's speed, which also frees a member that starts inside a solid (a hit at distance zero). It scales with the goal rather than the velocity, so a member slowed by a wall still resists a goal behind it. Always finite.
