ink/render/volumetrics

3 types · 38 values

Froxel volumetric fog (render.Fog, render.FogVolume), as the host's volume shaders compute it: the CPU reference for the grid's tiers and its depth slicing, the medium at a point (the global mist, its noise, the local volumes), the height mist's optical depth, the Henyey-Greenstein phase, a froxel's light source, the front to back integration of a column and the temporal blend. Pen's WGSL (volume_inject, volume_integrate) is held to these formulas and to an independent f64 reference. Nothing here keeps state.

Key items: Grid, Medium, Slice, exp, ln.

Types

data Grid 
type Grid is data

The froxel grid a quality tier asks for. width x height froxels across the picture and slices deep; filter is the sun's shadow filter in the volume (0 a single tap, 1 sixteen); octaves the global mist's noise octaves. Tier 0 is off (all zero); 1 is low and 2 high, as are all above it.

  • octaves: U32
data Medium 
type Medium is data

The medium at a point: its total extinction, the extinction that scatters by channel (scattering, extinction times albedo) and the light it gives out per unit of length (emitted, the sum of density times emission).

  • scattering: Vec3
  • emitted: Vec3

One slice of a column: its source term, its extinction and delta, the length of its segment along the ray.

const reach_ratio 
const reach_ratio: F32

The grid is sliced by the depth ahead of the camera along its view (not the distance along each ray), exponentially past a knee and almost linearly within it: boundary b of N has depth knee * (exp(b / N * span) - 1) with knee = distance / 64 and span = ln(1 + distance / knee) = ln 65, so the last boundary is exactly distance. Slice k lies between boundaries k and k + 1 and is sampled at boundary coordinate k + 0.5.

func noise_factor 
func noise_factor: Fog -> U32 -> Vec3 -> F32

The global mist's noise factor at p: max 0 (1 + noise * fbm q) with q the point carried by drift in units of noise_scale, so it varies the density between nothing (noise 1) and twice the mean while the mean stays what it was. Noise 0 is a factor of 1. octaves is the tier's.

func radial_mask

How much of the global mist's density reaches distance r from the vertical axis through the origin: 0 inside radius, 1 beyond it (smoothly over edge when that is above 0).

func global_density 
func global_density: Fog -> U32 -> Vec3 -> F32

The global mist's extinction at p per world unit: density, falling off with height above y = 0 over height (a positive number), cleared inside radius and varied by the noise.

func local_weight 
func local_weight: FogVolume -> Vec3 -> F32

A local volume's share of its density at p: with d = (p - centre) / extent by axis and m the box's largest |d| (shape 0) or the ellipsoid's |d|, the density rises over falloff of the half size in from the edge (t clamps (1 - m) / falloff to 0..1; a falloff of 0 is a hard edge, t 1 inside) with a smoothstep, t * t * (3 - 2 t). A half size of 0 or less holds nothing.

func medium_at 
func medium_at: Fog -> View FogVolume -> U32 -> Vec3 -> Medium

The global mist and every local volume at p: extinctions add, scattering adds sigma * albedo per channel, emission adds sigma * emission.

func height_optical_depth 
func height_optical_depth: Fog -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> F32

The global mist's optical depth along the straight segment from a to b, as the host's analytic fog integrates it (no radius, noise or local volumes): the exact integral of density * exp (-y / height) for the heights above 0 clamped at the ends, length * density * height * (exp(-ya / height) - exp(-yb / height)) / (yb - ya), and length * density * exp(-ya / height) when the heights are within 0.01. A segment crossing y = 0 is approximated.

func phase_scaled 
func phase_scaled: F32 -> F32 -> F32

pi times the Henyey-Greenstein phase function, (1 - g^2) / (4 (1 + g^2 - 2 g c)^1.5): g (clamped to +-0.95) is the asymmetry and c the cosine of the angle between the direction toward the light and the direction from the camera to the point (so g > 0 brightens looking toward the light).

func phase_hg 
func phase_hg: F32 -> F32 -> F32

The Henyey-Greenstein phase function itself, which integrates to 1 over the sphere.

func in_scatter 
func in_scatter: Vec3 -> F32 -> F32 -> F32 -> Vec3

The radiance a light of colour light gives a unit of scattering toward the eye: its colour times the scaled phase times its visibility (its shadow, 0 to 1, and whatever falloff the caller applies). The medium's scattering multiplies it, per channel, in scatter_source.

func sun_term 
func sun_term: Fog -> Vec3 -> F32 -> F32 -> Vec3

The sun's term: in_scatter with the mist's own anisotropy, scaled by fog.sun.

func ambient_term 
func ambient_term: Fog -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> Vec3

The ambient colour: the mean light all round blended toward the sky's own colour in the direction looked by fog.sky, scaled by fog.ambient.

func scatter_source 
func scatter_source: Medium -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> Vec3

A froxel's source term: the light the medium sends toward the eye per unit of length, scattering * (sun + ambient + lights) + emitted, by channel.

func slice_keep 
func slice_keep: Slice -> F32

func one_minus_exp

func slice_gain 
func slice_gain: Slice -> Vec3

The light a slice adds before its own attenuation: its source over its segment against its own transmittance, source * (1 - T) / extinction (source * delta when the extinction is vanishing, 1e-6 or less), exact however thick. The host evaluates 1 - T directly, which loses digits to cancellation when extinction * delta is under about 1e-3; this does not.

const column_stride
