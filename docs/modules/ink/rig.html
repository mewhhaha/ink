ink/rig

3 types · 15 values

Camera rigs: pure functions from caller-owned state to view.Camera poses. Cameras look down their local -Z with +Y up. Times are the caller's seconds, distances metres and angles radians; nothing here keeps a clock.

Key items: Follow, Follower, Aim, look_at, turn.

Types

data Follow 
type Follow is data

Follow-camera settings. The eye sits offset from the focus: along the world's axes, or, when turns, along the target's, turned by its heading, so (0, 2, 5) is above and behind a target facing -Z. The focus leads the target by its velocity times lookahead seconds, and moves only when that lead leaves the dead_zone radius (metres) around it. The focus and the eye spring after their goals at frequency (rad/s) and damping (a ratio), as motion.spring3; the rotation turns toward the focus at turn_rate, as turn.

data Follower 
type Follower is data

A follow camera's state, kept by the caller between steps. anchor is the dead zone's centre and seat the anchor plus the offset. focus springs after the anchor and eye after the seat, alike, so a fixed offset keeps its view while the camera follows.

func look_at 
func look_at: Vec3 -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> Camera

A camera at eye looking at target, keeping up up as math.look_rotation does. An eye on the target looks down -Z.

func turn 
func turn: Quat -> Quat -> F32 -> F32 -> Quat

current turned toward desired by exponential smoothing at rate per second: slerp by 1 - e^(-rate dt), so the same total time turns as far however it is split into steps. A rate or dt of zero or less, or NaN, keeps current; an infinite rate snaps to desired.

The rotation looking from eye at focus with +Y up, or fallback when they are within a tenth of a millimetre.

func follower

A follower already settled on a target at target, moving at velocity and facing heading (a yaw, 0 facing -Z, as math.Quat.heading), so the first step does not swoop in from elsewhere.

func chase 
func chase: Spring3 -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> F32 -> F32 -> F32 -> Spring3

motion.spring3 toward a goal moving steadily from from to to during the step, not held at to: the spring's steady lag behind a goal moving at rate r is 2 damping r / frequency, and what remains settles as toward a fixed goal. Exact for goals moving at constant speed, so following agrees across frame rates instead of drifting by half a step's travel. Works axis by axis on scalars, as motion.spring3 does, so the step builds no intermediate vectors.

func chase_axis 
func chase_axis: F32 -> F32 -> F32 -> F32 -> F32 -> F32 -> F32 -> F32 -> F32 -> Spring

One axis of chase: the goal moves at rate, (to - from) / dt, and the spring settles what remains beyond its steady lag, rate × gain.

func shown 
func shown: Follower -> Camera

The camera a follower shows.

func follow 
func follow: Follow -> Follower -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> F32 -> F32 -> (Follower, Camera)

One step of dt seconds after a target at target, moving at velocity and facing heading: the next state and its camera. When the lead point leaves the dead zone, the anchor is drawn after it, keeping it on the zone's edge. The springs chase the anchor and seat as moving steadily from their last places through the step. A dt of zero or less, or not finite, keeps the state, as does a non-finite lead for the anchor. After a cut or teleport, start a new follower.

func frame 
func frame: Lens -> F32 -> Quat -> #[Vec3] -> F32 -> Maybe Vec3

The eye position nearest the points, for a camera turned by rotation seeing through lens at aspect (width over height), that sees every point at least padding radians inside the frustum's sides and no nearer than the near plane. The points fill the padded view across one axis and are centred on the other. Nothing for no points, non-finite values, a zero rotation, a lens or aspect that is not positive, padding below zero or of half the field of view or more, or points too deep to fit before the far plane.

func collide 
func collide: Scene -> Vec3 -> Vec3 -> F32 -> U32 -> U32 -> Vec3

Where the eye should be so a sphere of radius around it stays out of solids: a sphere swept from pivot toward eye stops at the first solid it touches, and the way clear leaves eye unchanged. The cast uses the solid filter: mask layers, never entity skip (the followed body), no triggers. Any proxy counts, terrain included, which is usually wanted: keep the pivot more than the radius above the ground, as a sphere starting inside a solid stops at once and returns the pivot. Non-finite input returns eye.

func ahead 
func ahead: Spline -> F32 -> Quat

The rotation looking along path at distance, +Y up.

func dolly 
func dolly: Spline -> F32 -> Aim -> Camera

A camera distance metres along path, as spline.at, aimed by aim with +Y up. A camera on the point it looks at looks along the path; a fixed rotation is normalized.
