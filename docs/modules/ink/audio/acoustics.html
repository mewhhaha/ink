ink/audio/acoustics

8 types · 35 values

Deterministic multi-bounce acoustic ray tracing over ink/scene. Computes volumetric direct occlusion, 3-band wall transmission, 1-bounce edge diffraction (with apparent doorway direction), stochastic Eyring RT60 room acoustics, and stereo 3-band early reflection taps compatible with Pen's ACOUSTICS_EXT_TAG (65004) native & WebAudio convolution backend.

Key items: Material, Listener, Source, Config, uniform_material.

Types

data Material 
type Material is data

Physically-based 3-band acoustic surface material.

  • absorption_*: fraction of acoustic energy absorbed per bounce in [0, 1] (~250 Hz, ~1 kHz, ~4 kHz)
  • scattering: 0 = mirror specular reflection, 1 = diffuse Lambertian scattering
  • transmission: mid-band penetration through a barrier in [0, 1] (lows penetrate further, highs muffle faster)
