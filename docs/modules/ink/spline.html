ink/spline

1 types · 23 values

Splines: smooth curves through their points that cameras and other movers travel along by distance, at constant speed. The curve is centripetal Catmull-Rom (alpha 0.5): it passes through every point, with no cusps or self-intersections within a segment and no overshoot past close points. Distances are metres along the curve.

Key items: Spline, tangent_axis, norm3_scalar, knot, segment.

Types

data Spline 
type Spline is data

Each segment is a u^3 + b u^2 + c u + d for u in [0, 1], stored as twelve F32 coefficients, ax ay az bx by bz cx cy cz dx dy dz, in coefficients: reading them straight from one flat array keeps the hot arithmetic on scalars, with no vector allocated per step. lengths is the arc length from the start to each of samples equal steps of u in every segment, so it has a segment count times samples plus one entries, starting at zero and ending at length.

func parameter

The u, within the sample ending at table entry low, at distance s: two Newton steps on the arc length from the table's linear guess, so equal distances stay equal on the curve, not just on the table. One step leaves errors of a tenth of a percent at tight corners. The search and the steps return scalars, so finding a place allocates nothing.
