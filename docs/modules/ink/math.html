ink/math

8 types · 38 values

Ink's small geometry vocabulary. Vec2 and Vec3 come from std/vector with their operators; Vec4, Quat, Color and Ray are Ink's. Quaternions are [x, y, z, w] with w the scalar part.

Key items: Vec4, Quat, Color, Ray, vec2.

blot · runnable example
import * as math from "ink/math"
let q = math.axis_angle math.up (pi * 0.5)
let p = math.Quat.rotate q math.forward
p.x // => -1.0

Distance along the ray to the plane with normal through point, if the ray hits its front within reach.

A nonfinite coordinate needs no check of its own: times a zero it is NaN, otherwise infinite, so it makes facing or the distance nonfinite, and both are refused. With its checks in scalar helpers, the body stays small enough to inline into loops.

func rotate_parts 
func rotate_parts: F32 -> F32 -> F32 -> F32 -> F32 -> F32 -> F32 -> (F32, F32, F32)

Rotate a vector: q v q*, as v + t w + u × t with u = q.xyz and t = 2 (u × v). rotate_parts is one body: never inlined, it allocates only its tuple result (a Vec3 result would cost two allocations), so loop-free kernels use it. turn splits off the cross products so that loops inline it whole, but outside loops each of its helpers allocates; the Quat members use it. Both give the same bits.
