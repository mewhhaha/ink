ink/timeline

8 types · 27 values

A timeline is keyed tracks and timed cues over a caller-owned Playback. Times are local seconds from the timeline's start. Each frame the caller advances the playback by its own seconds, samples tracks at position and fires cues over the passes the advance returns. Nothing here keeps a clock.

Key items: Key, Track, Cue, Cues, track.

func advance 
func advance: Playback -> F32 -> Maybe Advanced

Advances by dt seconds at the playback's speed, returning the traversed passes in order. A pass ends where its traversal arrives and includes its start only after play, seek or a loop restart, so each instant is passed once: a loop restart passes the start again, a ping-pong turn passes its end once. Refuses an invalid playback, a negative or non-finite dt, or more whole traversals than F32 counts. A paused playback or zero travel moves nowhere.

func fired

The values of the cues the passes cross, in traversal order: a forward pass fires cues in (from, to], a backward pass in [to, from), and an inclusive pass also those at from. Equal-time cues fire in list order forwards and in reverse backwards. The output is allocated once at its exact size.
