ink/mesh

8 types · 31 values

Static triangle meshes: finite, world-space, double-sided collision surfaces for level geometry, with the segment/triangle primitives under them.

A mesh is cooked once from a triangle soup into flat pools: the vertices, a seven-word record per triangle (three vertex ids, edge/vertex flags and the three edge neighbours), a unit plane per triangle and a BVH over the triangle bounds. Triangles with a repeated or collinear vertex are dropped and equal positions are welded, so duplicated vertices still share edges.

Internal edges: every shared edge records how its two triangles meet. A contact on an edge or vertex of triangle A is 2 (active: a real convex feature seen from the body's side), 1 (smooth: the neighbours are within five degrees of coplanar, so the face normal replaces the edge normal) or 0 (concave: the neighbour's face covers it). Boundary and non-manifold edges are always active. Bodies sliding over a tessellated surface therefore feel its faces, not the seams.

Key items: Point3, Plane4, Span, Stored, unpack.
